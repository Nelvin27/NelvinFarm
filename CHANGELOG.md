# Changelog

Player-facing changes to Clover Patch 3D, newest first. Technical detail is in the [work log](docs/agent-work/CLOVER-VALLEY.md).

## 2026-11-13

### Added
- Council Row beside Market Square, with the petition boards, the town bell, and Iona's house.
- Bread Lane from the bakery to the commons, with Mara's house on it.
- The clinic, the old floodgate with a path along the brook, and the ruins of three cottages lost to the flood.
- Four seasonal music themes that change with the season.
- Run with Shift. Zoom the camera with Shift + mouse wheel.
- Left-click to use what you face; right-click to use the selected hotbar item.
- A Sort bag button, and right-click or double-click to use an item in the bag.

### Changed
- E now picks up the right tool for the plot or dig spot you face.
- Holding E while walking works each plot in a row once.
- When a seed runs out, the farmer moves on to another in-season seed.
- You can go to bed from 18:00 without being tired.
- All sound effects are remade to match the new music.
- The orchard moved east, and the Hilltop Trail now loops behind it.

### Removed
- The single looping theme, replaced by the seasonal themes.

## 2026-11-12

### Added
- Highwater Brook, with four bridges.
- Signs can be read; petition boards post a daily notice.
- Trees can be shaken, ducks fed, the wishing well wished on, and the town bell rung.
- Buried finds to dig up, four each day.
- 16 places to discover, counted on the Collection page.

### Fixed
- Switching tools while standing on a buried find now takes effect at once.
- A gap in the outer mountains that could let the farmer reach the map edge.

## 2026-11-09

### Added
- A rebuilt Clover Valley: Market Square, the commons, the orchard, three fishing waters, lookouts, and hidden places.
- Lake Trout, Reed Carp, and the rare Moonfin.
- Seasonal forage: Wild Berries, Chanterelles, and River Shells.
- A nine-specimen collection.
- Lantern light at night, puddles in the rain, and snow in winter.

### Changed
- The cast is reduced to Iona and Mara for now. The other residents will return.
- The game's scenes are authored in the Godot editor, so they can be edited there.
