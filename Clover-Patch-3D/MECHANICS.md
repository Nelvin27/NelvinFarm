# Clover Patch mechanics comparison

This inventory covers the major player-facing systems in Stardew Valley through the 1.6 update family and the original PlayStation release of Harvest Moon: Back to Nature. The comparison uses developer publications, the PlayStation manual, and community references for detailed rules. It is a documentation-based behavior study. It does not establish executable internals, exact random distributions, regional differences, every event trigger, or every item and recipe. The PlayStation rerelease's rewind, quick save, and filters belong to its emulation layer. [Stardew Valley overview](https://www.stardewvalley.net/about/) · [1.6 changelog](https://www.stardewvalley.net/stardew-valley-1-6-update-full-changelog/) · [Original PlayStation manual](https://oldgamesdownload.com/wp-content/uploads/Harvest_Moon_Back_to_Nature_Manual_PS_EN.pdf#page=1) · [PlayStation rerelease](https://store.playstation.com/en-us/concept/10005231)

**Live** means a playable system exists in Clover Patch. **Partial** means a related system exists and the proposed extension is still open. **Planned** means the system remains to be built. Designs in the last column are Clover Patch proposals. The [player guide](README.md) describes the controls and currently playable features.

## Current gameplay similarity

Each applicable category gets **1** for a comparable core loop, **½** for a simplified or narrower version, or **0** when Clover Patch has no playable equivalent. A dash excludes a category outside that game's comparison scope. Categories count equally. Divide the points earned by the applicable categories and round to the nearest whole percent.

## Farm, calendar, and economy

| System | Stardew Valley | Back to Nature | Clover Patch merge |

| Day and seasons | The clock drives activities, opening hours, and travel. Each season changes the available crops and activities. [Manual, pp. 9–10, 18](https://oldgamesdownload.com/wp-content/uploads/Harvest_Moon_Back_to_Nature_Manual_PS_EN.pdf#page=1) | **Live.** Keep Clover Patch's 30-day seasons, live clock, sleep transition, and dawn autosave. **Planned.** Show event dates, birthdays, and opening hours in the journal. |
| Weather and recovery | Rain changes watering, fish availability, and some NPC schedules. Food restores energy or health, and some meals give temporary bonuses. [Fishing](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Fishing) · [Cooking](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Cooking) | Rain covers watering and storms affect work. Food and hot springs restore stamina. Power Berries increase stamina permanently. Add restorative places and permanent upgrades before considering harsher weather penalties. |
| Crop cycle | Seasonal seeds, watered growth, repeat harvests, fertilizer, quality, and farm infrastructure support larger harvests. [Overview](https://www.stardewvalley.net/about/) · [1.6 changelog](https://www.stardewvalley.net/stardew-valley-1-6-update-full-changelog/) | Players hoe, sow, water, and harvest. Seed bags cover an area, some crops regrow, and sickles cut grass. [Manual, pp. 13–14](https://oldgamesdownload.com/wp-content/uploads/Harvest_Moon_Back_to_Nature_Manual_PS_EN.pdf#page=1) | **Partial.** Retain single-plot sowing, crop timing, repeat harvests, fodder, and seasonal wilting. The watering can can be refilled and upgraded to cover more tiles. [Manual, pp. 8, 14, 16–17](https://oldgamesdownload.com/wp-content/uploads/Harvest_Moon_Back_to_Nature_Manual_PS_EN.pdf#page=1) | **Partial.** Hoe, sickle, can, and the adjacent-plot can upgrade already work. Add more tool tiers alongside mining and a smith. Sprinklers belong after manual irrigation has a clear upgrade path. |
| Shipping and stores | Shops can pay immediately, while shipping pays the following morning. [Manual, pp. 10, 17](https://oldgamesdownload.com/wp-content/uploads/Harvest_Moon_Back_to_Nature_Manual_PS_EN.pdf#page=1) | **Live.** Keep the immediate market, per-item-type cottage bin, return-before-sleep action, and dawn payout. **Planned.** Add a journal ledger for daily shipments, prices, and sale totals. |
| Bag and storage | Backpack upgrades, chests, inventory stacks, and specialist shops support different activities. [Getting started](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Getting_Started) · [1.6 changelog](https://www.stardewvalley.net/stardew-valley-1-6-update-full-changelog/) | The rucksack can be upgraded. Add persistent cottage storage and filters for ingredients, products, and materials. |
| Foraging and trees | Seasonal pickups, woodcutting, and other activities feed a foraging skill and crafting. [Foraging](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Foraging) | Forest food varies seasonally. [Manual, pp. 9, 19](https://oldgamesdownload.com/wp-content/uploads/Harvest_Moon_Back_to_Nature_Manual_PS_EN.pdf#page=1) | **Partial.** The existing apple orchard rotates ripe trees in fall. Add seasonal meadow and woodland pickups, renewable lumber, and honey tied to nearby flowers. |
| Construction and layout | Choose a farm layout, build or move farm structures, renovate the home, and decorate. [Farm maps](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Farm_Maps) · [1.5 changelog](https://www.stardewvalley.net/stardew-valley-1-5-update-full-changelog/) | The carpenter uses wood and money to remodel farm buildings and the house. [Manual, pp. 10–12, 18](https://oldgamesdownload.com/wp-content/uploads/Harvest_Moon_Back_to_Nature_Manual_PS_EN.pdf#page=1) | **Partial.** Cottage repair already changes the exterior and interior. ## Fishing, livestock, and production

| System | Stardew Valley | Back to Nature | Clover Patch merge |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rod fishing | Cast, hook a bite, then keep a moving fish inside a controllable zone. Water body, time, season, and weather affect catches. [Fishing](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Fishing) | Obtain a rod at the beach and respond to a bite. The catch can be put into the farm pond. [Manual, pp. 10, 17–18](https://oldgamesdownload.com/wp-content/uploads/Harvest_Moon_Back_to_Nature_Manual_PS_EN.pdf#page=1) · [Contemporary walkthrough](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps/446412-harvest-moon-back-to-nature/faqs/10372) | **Live.** Willow Pond has a timed bite. Pond Perch goes into the bag and can be eaten, cooked, sold, or shipped. **Planned.** Add fishing tackle, catch records, more water bodies, and rarer catches. |
| Fish keeping | Build fish ponds with species-specific production and capacity milestones. Tanks display catches. [Fish ponds](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Fish_Pond) · [1.5 changelog](https://www.stardewvalley.net/stardew-valley-1-5-update-full-changelog/) | Stock the farm pond with caught fish and feed them to grow the stock. [Manual, pp. 9–10](https://oldgamesdownload.com/wp-content/uploads/Harvest_Moon_Back_to_Nature_Manual_PS_EN.pdf#page=1) | **Planned.** Make a separate stocked pond with daily feed, a population cap, and harvestable stock. |
| Livestock | Coops and barns house animals. Feeding, petting, and access to grass affect production, with some animals producing higher-quality goods at higher friendship. [Animals](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Animals) | Feed and care for chickens, cows, and sheep. Collect eggs, milk, and wool. Building upgrades expand capacity and enable processing. [Manual, pp. 10, 15–17](https://oldgamesdownload.com/wp-content/uploads/Harvest_Moon_Back_to_Nature_Manual_PS_EN.pdf#page=1) | **Planned.** Start with a coop, a hen, daily feeding and petting, and eggs that enter the same food and shipping inventory. Add barn species, grazing, breeding, and care events after the coop loop works. |
| Pets and transport | Pets build friendship, and the horse provides transport. Updates expanded pet adoption and interaction. [1.6 changelog](https://www.stardewvalley.net/stardew-valley-1-6-update-full-changelog/) | Care for a dog that helps protect the farm and a horse that participates in its own events. [Manual, pp. 10, 16–17](https://oldgamesdownload.com/wp-content/uploads/Harvest_Moon_Back_to_Nature_Manual_PS_EN.pdf#page=1) | **Planned.** Add companion care and a mount once the map has enough travel to make it useful. |
| Crafting and processing | Recipes use materials to make tools, equipment, and machines. Machines turn raw goods into products with different values. [Overview](https://www.stardewvalley.net/about/) · [1.6 changelog](https://www.stardewvalley.net/stardew-valley-1-6-update-full-changelog/) | Farm equipment processes eggs, milk, wool, and feed. Machines are associated with farm upgrades and the blacksmith. [Manual, pp. 10, 15–18](https://oldgamesdownload.com/wp-content/uploads/Harvest_Moon_Back_to_Nature_Manual_PS_EN.pdf#page=1) | **Planned.** Begin with a workbench, a small set of ore and wood recipes, and one processor for a farm product. Save queued work and outputs across dawn. |
| Cooking and recipes | Learn recipes and cook with ingredients. Meals can restore resources or apply buffs. [Cooking](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Cooking) | Expand the house, obtain kitchen equipment, and record recipes. Ingredients and utensils matter. [Manual, pp. 11–12](https://oldgamesdownload.com/wp-content/uploads/Harvest_Moon_Back_to_Nature_Manual_PS_EN.pdf#page=1) | **Live.** Cottage repair unlocks three recipes using bread, turnips, apples, and fish. Cooking serves the meal immediately and restores food and fatigue. **Planned.** Add eggs and teach recipes through villagers and the journal. |

## Animal Crossing and Rune Factory 4

The Valley Collection adapts the donation loop from Animal Crossing: New Horizons. Clover Patch records three donated specimens in the journal and awards coins when the set is complete. [Nintendo's guide to collecting and donations](https://animalcrossing.nintendo.com/new-horizons/explore/)

Practice-based skills adapt Rune Factory 4's links between everyday activities and progression. Plot work, successful catches, and cooking improve separate skills. Each skill improves that activity in Clover Patch.
